HUONG DAN SOAN BAI HTML CHO GAME LEO NUI
=========================================

Y tuong: ban nho Claude soan 1 file HTML bai tap. Khi hoc sinh tra loi DUNG, bai goi
climb.correct() -> nhan vat cua em do leo len tren man chieu. Tra loi SAI thi goi
climb.wrong() (khong leo, chi dem so cau sai).

Game tu dong tiem san doi tuong "climb" vao bai khi hoc sinh mo bai, nen bai chi can goi.

----- LOI NHAC MAU DE DAN CHO CLAUDE (thay phan trong [ ]) -----

Hay soan cho toi 1 file HTML duy nhat (HTML + CSS + JavaScript trong cung 1 file) la bai tap
tieng Anh: [chu de, vi du: 15 cau chia dong tu thi hien tai don, trinh do lop 6].
Yeu cau ky thuat:
1. Dau phan <head> co dong nay (de bai van chay khi mo rieng):
   <script>window.climb = window.climb || { correct: function(){}, wrong: function(){} };</script>
2. Moi khi hoc sinh tra loi dung 1 cau, goi window.climb.correct() DUNG 1 LAN cho cau do.
   Moi khi tra loi sai, goi window.climb.wrong() 1 lan. Moi cau chi tinh 1 lan (khoa cau
   sau khi da tra loi, hoac chi tinh lan dau).
3. Khong dung alert(), confirm(), prompt(); khong dung localStorage/sessionStorage/cookie;
   khong goi mang ra ngoai (font, thu vien tu CDN...). Moi thu nam trong file.
4. Giao dien rong toi da khoang 800px, chu to de doc tren dien thoai.
5. Bai co dung [so cau] cau.

----- KHI DUNG TRONG GAME -----
- Trang giao vien: chon "Bai HTML cua toi", chon file, dat "So cau dung de len dinh" = so cau
  cua bai (hoac nho hon neu muon len dinh som), bam Tao phong.
- File mau de thu: samples/bai-mau.html (5 cau).
- Bai tai len chi ton tai trong phong do; muon dung lai o lan sau thi tai lai file.
